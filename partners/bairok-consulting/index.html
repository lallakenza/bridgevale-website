Partnership Governance

How a Joint Engagement Works

A typical sequence when Bridgevale and Bairok work together. The exact shape of an engagement is agreed with the client before any work begins.

  1. 01

    Joint opportunity assessment

    Both firms review the context together to establish what is actually needed, which capabilities are relevant and whether a joint engagement is the right answer.

  2. 02

    Strategy and transformation framing

    Bridgevale leads the framing work — objectives, scope, operating model and the decisions that need to be settled before design begins.

  3. 03

    Specialist SAP and AI delivery

    Bairok contributes specialist SAP expertise to design and delivery, including where AI and automation are being considered alongside the ERP scope.

  4. 04

    Governance, quality assurance and executive alignment

    Bridgevale maintains programme governance, reviews deliverables and keeps executive stakeholders aligned with what is being built.

  5. 05

    Knowledge transfer and long-term enablement

    Working sessions, walkthroughs and documentation are used to transfer knowledge to internal teams so the organisation retains ownership after the engagement.
